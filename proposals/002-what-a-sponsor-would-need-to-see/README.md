## <img src="../assets/icons/compass.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> TL;DR

> [!TIP]
> **Five lines, if that's all you read tonight.**
>
> 1. <img src="../assets/icons/eye.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **A sponsor reads the record before the pitch.** The repos, the changelog, the decision log, the audits, the entity papers and the counsel's opinion are the application; the deck comes after.
> 2. <img src="../assets/icons/status-ready.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **On the shelf today:** an open, dated code base with contracts and a dApp; a public-preview pipeline that keeps private material private; a fleet that commits with attribution; a sister initiative (psychedelic-policy-reform) with a live log; and a lab that already runs a public, point-in-time trading record.
> 3. <img src="../assets/icons/status-partly.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Half there:** the whitepaper (three sources to reconcile, proposal 001); the contracts review before any testnet; the governance that honours quorum, written but not yet enforced on-chain.
> 4. <img src="../assets/icons/status-wait.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Not there yet, and not ours to rush:** the entity structure and counsel's written opinion; an audit; a treasury policy; a named steward team with capacity.
> 5. <img src="../assets/icons/hand-heart.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **The framing that fits:** a framework and worked example for DAO-governed spiritual and recovery communities, built in public, that others can fork. Sponsorship would fund building at scale with others, not a token sale.

## <img src="../assets/icons/route.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> The order that gets the shelf full

1. <img src="../assets/icons/book-open.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Reconcile the whitepaper (proposal 001) and publish the three-tier map.
2. <img src="../assets/icons/scale.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Counsel: entity structure and a written view on the core tier. Only then does the reframed tier get a question each.
3. <img src="../assets/icons/shield-check.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Contracts review, then testnet, then an audit of the core tier only.
4. <img src="../assets/icons/landmark.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Governance on-chain for the core: proposals, quorum, roles, a treasury with reporting.
5. <img src="../assets/icons/users.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Community feedback and the first trusted servants.
6. <img src="../assets/icons/hand-heart.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Then, and only then, a conversation with a launchpad or sponsor, with this shelf as the application.

## <img src="../assets/icons/message-circle-question.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Questions to settle before any sponsor conversation

| Question | Why it comes first |
|---|---|
| What is being sponsored: the church's tooling, a foundation's open framework, or a DAO? | A sponsor funds an entity; the drafts offer three options and counsel has not chosen |
| What does the sponsor receive? | Nothing that looks like an investment contract; grants, services and attribution are the shapes to explore with counsel |
| Who signs? | The steward team and the roles the governance names; not one person |
| What is public before the conversation? | Everything on the "ready" rows above; nothing from the private lanes |
