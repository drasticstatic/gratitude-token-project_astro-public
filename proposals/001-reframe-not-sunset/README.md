## <img src="../assets/icons/compass.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> TL;DR

> [!TIP]
> **Five lines, if that's all you read tonight.**
>
> 1. <img src="../assets/icons/book-open.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **There are three sources, and they disagree.** The ChatGPT-era whitepaper the Docusaurus site was built from; Christopher's pen-and-paper edits of October 2025 (the only source in his own hand, not yet transcribed); and six claude.ai drafts from October 2026 that reframe some ideas and cut others.
> 2. <img src="../assets/icons/git-branch.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **The drafts fork against themselves.** One keeps ETHO, a donation-with-token campaign, liquidity pools, a community-governed trading agent and an exchange, all reframed as active stewardship. Another keeps three tokens and removes all of that. The MOU and the timeline assume the tokens the simpler model deletes.
> 3. <img src="../assets/icons/sprout.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Christopher's stance: reframe and repurpose, don't sunset.** Ideas that arrived are kept and shown as what is possible; what ships first is a smaller, counsel-cleared core; the rest waits in a clearly labelled "later, if cleared" tier rather than a deleted section.
> 4. <img src="../assets/icons/landmark.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Governance is the thread that holds it:** proposals and decisions that honour quorum, role-based where a decision needs it, so the community feels involved and the record stays professional. That is true whichever tier a feature sits in.
> 5. <img src="../assets/icons/list-checks.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **The ask of Christopher** is one afternoon: mark each draft section keep / reframe / later / unsure, transcribe the hand edits, and answer the four questions the chat left open. Agents format everything after that.

## <img src="../assets/icons/sprout.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> The proposal: three tiers, one document

Instead of choosing between the two drafts, lay every idea on one map with three tiers. Nothing is deleted; everything is placed.

| Tier | What goes here | How it is shown |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="../assets/icons/status-ready.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Core, ships first** | what both drafts agree on and counsel is most likely to clear: earn-only GRAT, soulbound Proof of Burn and Role NFTs, ceremonial burning to a transparent treasury, DAO governance as church polity, church-held real estate with usage rights | the whitepaper's main body; the first contracts; the first dojo of the dApp |
| <img src="../assets/icons/status-partly.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Reframed, pending counsel** | the ideas the drafts reframe rather than cut: the donation campaign, stewardship pools, the community-governed treasury agent, member-to-member exchange, the dual treasury | a "What is possible" section, each with the Howey argument the draft makes and the question for counsel beside it; built only after a written opinion |
| <img src="../assets/icons/status-wait.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Later, with trusted servants** | what needs people and capacity before it needs code: multiple properties, international chapters, an open-source toolkit for other communities, research partnerships | a roadmap tier with no dates, revisited when the community appoints or employs trusted servants to help carry it |

The map shows the whole vision to anyone who reads it, which is the point: a framework and an example for others in the blockchain space to see what is possible in a DAO-governed community. It also gives counsel a clean question per idea instead of one large yes-or-no.

## <img src="../assets/icons/landmark.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Governance as the constant

Whatever tier a feature sits in, the way decisions are made stays the same, and that is what makes the whole thing feel both involving and professional:

- <img src="../assets/icons/users.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Proposals honour quorum.** A decision counts when enough of the community has taken part, and the record shows who did. Eventually that is enforced by the contracts themselves, not by goodwill.
- <img src="../assets/icons/key-round.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Role-based where it needs to be.** Treasury movements, property matters and partnerships pass through the roles the simplified model already names (treasury guardians, ministers, flamekeepers), with term limits and removal by vote.
- <img src="../assets/icons/eye.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Everything on the record.** Proposals, votes, allocations and outcomes are public on-chain and summarised in the Astro sidecar's changelog, so a newcomer or a sponsor can read the history in an afternoon.
- <img src="../assets/icons/hand-heart.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Capacity decides pace.** Nothing in a later tier moves until there are people to manage it. Weeding happens by the community's feedback, not by a draft's deletion.
